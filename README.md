# Interview Questions

This repo defines a FastAPI app with two constructs: a `User` and an `Organization`.

## Pre-interview

1. Rate your understanding and experience in FastAPI from a 1 to 10.

2. Rate your understanding and experience with MongoDB from a 1 to 10.

3. Rate your understanding and experience with `pytest` from a 1 to 10.

## Interview

1. Explain to me the schemas of a `User` and `Organization`.
> Can you think of any advantages of this design?
> Can you think of any disadvantages of this design?

2. Complete the task in `test_orgs.py`.
> Pay attention to `conftest.py` as this acts as a configuration file for `pytest`.
